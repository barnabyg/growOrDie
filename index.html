How to play

Each Year, plan production and investment, then allocate the actual Surplus between Storage and automatic Export. Preparing new land and purchasing Technologies improve future Years. Storage buffers food shortfalls but costs annual upkeep and cannot be exported. Export income funds future investment.

Meeting Consumption grows Population. Famine reduces it. Score is your peak Population; every doubling earns a Milestone. Collapse ends the run below half the starting Population. There is no fixed winning Population.

Feed your people before growing your country: fertilizing cultivated land raises Yield, though weather can still reduce it. The food outlook is an estimate; the Event is rolled when you resolve the Harvest. Plan spending includes the mandatory upkeep for surviving old food that must stay in Storage; you choose how much new food to keep after the Harvest.

Technologies are one-off purchases whose effects start the next Year. Owned Technologies appear as landmarks in your country.

Only new Harvest can be exported; food in Storage never leaves the country. Progress saves after resolving the Harvest and finishing the Year. A resolved Harvest is committed: Reloading resumes the allocation with the same Event. Sound is optional and starts off; motion follows your device's reduced-motion preference.

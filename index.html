    How to play

    Each Year, plan production and investment, then allocate the actual Surplus between Storage and automatic Export. Preparing new land and purchasing Technologies improve future Years. Storage buffers food shortfalls but costs annual upkeep and cannot be exported.

    Meeting Consumption grows Population. Famine reduces it. Score is your peak Population; every doubling earns a Milestone. Collapse ends the run below half the starting Population. There is no fixed winning Population.

    Progress saves after resolving the Harvest and finishing the Year. Reloading preserves a committed Event. Sound is optional and starts off; motion follows your device's reduced-motion preference.
